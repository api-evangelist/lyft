# Lyft

> Lyft is a transportation network company operating ride-hailing, bikeshare and scooter services across
> the United States and Canada. Its API surface has two halves that behave very differently: a
> login-gated ride-hailing developer program (Concierge and Rides), and a wide-open, anonymously
> readable GBFS micromobility data surface on gbfs.lyft.com.

## Agent notes

- POST /rides and POST /concierge/rides have NO idempotency key. A retried create dispatches a second
  driver and charges a second fare. Do not retry blindly.
- Both ride surfaces can be reversed with a cancel operation, but no numeric free-cancellation window is
  published; a fee "may apply depending on how long the driver has been en route".
- No 429 and no Retry-After is declared anywhere. Back off on your own schedule.
- GBFS feeds advertise ttl: 60. Honour it; do not poll faster.
- The lyft_den GBFS feed has not updated since 2024-12-16 despite advertising ttl 60. Treat it as stale.
